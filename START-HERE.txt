MANZ MUSIC PLAYER (Windows 11)
==============================

1. Double-click launch-windows.bat.
2. Wait while it sets up (first run only) and opens your browser.
3. In the app, click "+ Add music folder" and pick your music folder.

Keep the black launcher window open while using the app.
Closing that window stops the server.

Phone or another PC on the same network:
Run launch-windows-lan.bat, allow the Windows Firewall prompt, then open
http://<this-PC-ip>:8642 on the other device. See README.md for details.

Shortcut on the Desktop:
Run create-desktop-shortcut.bat once. That shortcut starts LAN mode, so the
same click works on this PC and on your phone (firewall prompt appears once).

Your data (library index, settings, cover cache):
%LOCALAPPDATA%\ManzMusicPlayer

Troubleshooting and full instructions: README.md

Updates:
The newest version (UI, fixes, new features) is published on GitHub.
See the "Project home and updates" section at the top of README.md.
